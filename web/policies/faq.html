Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

Use the Track Order option and enter the FASHIONOID order number shown after checkout.

Can I cancel an order?

Cancellation depends on the order status and the final FASHIONOID cancellation policy.

How do returns work?

See the Returns & Refunds page for the final eligibility rules and request process.

Which payment methods are supported?

Online payment methods will depend on the payment gateway enabled for the production store. COD can be offered subject to the final business rules.

How do I contact support?

Use the Contact page for the official FASHIONOID support details.
